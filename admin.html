Holiday Looks

Save holiday versions of our logos and flying pig. Give a look its dates once and it switches on by itself every year, then back to the normal look.

Tip: use PNG pictures with a see-through background so they sit nicely on the black. Only one look shows at a time; if two have the same dates, the one higher in the list wins.

Site Photos

Every photo on the website, by where it shows. Changes show on the site within a minute.

How to: Change a photo: press Replace under it and pick the new one. Go back: Use the original puts the old photo back. Our Work: add photos with the button above; Remove takes one off (you can put it back). Captions: type in the box under a photo; it saves when you click away. Groups: change the group box under a photo to move it. To rename a whole group, pick it in All groups, then Rename This Group.
