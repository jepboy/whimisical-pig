Honoring service

Military framing, done right

For military members, retirees, and former service members: framing, shadow boxes, plaques, and awards designed to be presented. Bring in your medals, flag, photos, and patches and we'll build a piece worth hanging.

  • Retirement & farewell shadow boxes
  • Flag cases & flag shadow boxes
  • Regulation & non-regulation guidons
  • Battalion spreads & framed military artwork
  • 10th Mountain snowshoes, plaques & awards

How It Works

  1. Bring it in

    Stop by the shop or call with your idea. Bring the medals, flag, photos, or artwork you want to feature.

  2. Design it together

    We help you pick the layout, frame, and finishing touches, and you approve the design before we build.

  3. Pick it up

    Your finished piece is ready to hang, gift, or present at the ceremony.
