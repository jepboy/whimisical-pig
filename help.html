Mailing Your Items

Not in the Watertown area? Here's how to get your items to us safely.

  1. Start your order online first.Then email us at WhimsicalPig@hotmail.com with your order reference so we know your package is coming.
  2. Keep a record.Photograph every item and keep a list of what's in the box.
  3. Wrap each item separately.Use acid-free tissue or soft cloth, then bubble wrap. Never tape directly to medals, ribbons or fabric.
  4. Keep flags and documents flat or folded as they are.Slip documents into a sleeve between two pieces of stiff cardboard.
  5. Use a sturdy box with padding on all sides so nothing shifts when you shake it.
  6. Ship with tracking and insurance.Put your name, phone and order reference on a note inside the box.
Ship to The Whimsical Pig · 50 Public Square, Suite E1 · Watertown, NY 13601

Caring for Your Piece

A few simple habits keep framed pieces looking their best for years.

Where to hang it

  • Out of direct sunlight, which fades fabric, photos and ribbons
  • Away from heat vents, fireplaces and damp rooms like bathrooms
  • On an inside wall when you can, where temperature stays steady

Hanging & cleaning

  • Use hardware rated for the weight; shadow boxes are heavier than they look
  • Dust the frame with a soft, dry cloth
  • Spray glass cleaner on the cloth, never directly on the glass
