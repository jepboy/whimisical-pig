Downtown on Public Square

The Whimsical Pig is a family owned and operated shop on Public Square in downtown Watertown, New York. We've been here since 2003, and four generations of our family have been part of the story.

Our specialty is military framing: shadow boxes, flag cases, guidons and battalion spreads that honor service members, retirees and the families who love them. We also do custom framing for art and keepsakes, embroidery and printing, awards and plaques, and personalized gifts.

And yes, there's a pig. A little whimsy goes a long way, even alongside the most meaningful pieces we make. Our goal is simple: make every order feel “too easy.”
